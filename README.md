# Velocity Indonesian

**56 Indonesian phrases in a ~10-minute lesson - a brand-new video every day.**

Velocity Indonesian is a daily Indonesian lesson for English speakers. Every episode is built from
scratch: fresh topics, fresh phrases, natural Bahasa Indonesia audio and an English pronunciation
guide for every line. Watch, repeat out loud, and grow your Indonesian one chapter at a time.

## What is inside every lesson

- **56 real-life phrases** across **8 themed chapters**
- **Native audio** for every Indonesian line
- **English pronunciation** with stressed syllables in CAPITALS
- A **new topic mix every day** - no repeated phrase lists
- A clean 16:9 lesson built for phones, tablets and TVs

## Daily schedule

A new lesson is published **every day** on the Velocity Indonesian YouTube channel
(@velocityindonesian). Each video stays under 15 minutes and is designed for a single commute.

## Topics

Motivation, travel, food, work, daily routine, feelings, making plans, sports,
celebrations, friendship, confidence, kindness and many more - 50
topics rotate so the channel never repeats itself.

## Built with

Python, FFmpeg, edge-tts, Playwright and Pillow. The renderer produces deterministic
1920x1080 frames, an original music bed, motion graphics and six thumbnail variations
for every lesson.
